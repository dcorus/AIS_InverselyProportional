\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
The proof follows the same template as that of Theorem~\ref{expohdOnLO}
({\expoF} on \textsc{LeadingOnes}), with one additional ingredient: the
mutation potential $M = n^{H(x,\text{opt})/n}$ is now \emph{random}, since
the Hamming distance $H(x,\text{opt}) = 1 + B$ with
$B\sim\mathrm{Bin}(n{-}i{-}1,\,1/2)$ depends on the uniformly distributed
tail bits.  A Chernoff bound shows that $|B - (n{-}i{-}1)/2| \le n^{2/3}$
throughout the run w.o.p.; since $n^{\pm n^{-1/3}} = 1 \pm o(1)$, this
gives $M = (1\pm o(1))\,n^{h_i}$ with
$h_i := (n{-}i{+}1)/(2n) \approx 1/2 - i/(2n)$.
The per-level recurrence then yields the same closed form
$E(f_i) = i\cdot M + n - i$ as in Theorem~\ref{expohdOnLO}, and summing
over all levels reduces to the series
$\sum_{i=1}^{n-1} i\,y^i$ with $y := n^{-1/(2n)}$ (compare
$x = n^{-1/n}$ in Theorem~\ref{expohdOnLO}).  Applying
$1 - y = (\ln n)/(2n)\cdot(1{+}o(1))$ via Lemma~\ref{lem:expo} and the
identity $\sum i\,y^i = y/(1{-}y)^2$ gives
$\sum i\,y^i = \Theta(n^2/\ln^2 n)$.  The halved exponent in~$y$ produces
a prefactor of~$n^{1/2}$ (versus~$n$ for {\expoF}), yielding
$S = \Theta(n^{5/2}/\log^2 n)$.  The upper and lower bound arguments
are identical to those of Theorem~\ref{expohdOnLO}.
The complete proof is given in Appendix~\ref{app:thm5}.
\end{proof}
